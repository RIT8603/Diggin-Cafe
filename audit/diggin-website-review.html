Website review

diggin.co.in: what it costs you today

Site www.diggin.co.in Checked 19 Oct 2026 Pages Home, Menu Device Mobile

The current site has warm photography and a clear mood. But the Book Now button doesn’t take bookings, the menu shows template prices, and the five outlets have no pages of their own. Guests already love Diggin (4.3★ from 16,037 Google reviews). The website doesn’t turn that interest into tables.

Lab test with Lighthouse 12.2 on a simulated mid-range phone. The current homepage was tested live; the redesign was tested as a production build with stock photos. Scores move a little between runs and will change once the real photos are added.

Findings

12 issues, most serious first

  1. Critical

    “Book Now” doesn’t book anything

    The button in the header links back to the homepage (href="https://www.diggin.co.in"). There is no booking form anywhere on the site. Every guest who taps it ends up where they started, and some of them will book somewhere else.

    Fix: a booking request form with outlet, date, time and party size, sent to the outlet team for confirmation by phone or WhatsApp.

  2. Critical

    Menu shows template prices: ₹4.00 and ₹7.00

    Chocolate mousse is listed at “Single serve ₹4.00, Double serve ₹7.00”. No other dish has a price. The dessert photos (plates on a pale blue background) also look like the website builder’s sample content. Please check whether these desserts are on the current menu.

    Fix: replace with the real menu, real prices or no prices, and veg / non-veg marks.

  3. High

    No pizzas or pastas on the online menu

    The homepage promotes “handcrafted pastas, stone-baked pizzas”. The menu page lists only 15 dishes: Soups (4), Salads (5) and Desserts (6). Guests searching for Diggin’s pizza can’t find it.

    Fix: publish the complete menu by category, easy to update when dishes change.

  4. High

    Menu page is titled “Menu | My Site 2”

    This leftover name from the website template appears in the browser tab, in shared links and in Google search results.

    Fix: proper titles and descriptions on every page, e.g. “Menu: Italian & Continental | Diggin Cafe, Delhi”.

  5. High

    Slow on mobile: 7.7 seconds to show the main image

    The homepage downloads 8.3 MB and takes 9.5 seconds to become usable on a mid-range phone. Most visitors arrive on mobile data from Google Maps or Instagram.

    Fix: correctly sized, compressed images and a lightweight build. The demo loads 0.4 MB.

  6. High

    Five outlets, no outlet pages

    Anand Lok, Chanakyapuri, Bikaner House, Connaught Place, Gardin by Diggin and the upcoming Tagore Garden appear only as names linking to Google Maps. None has an address, opening hours or its own phone number on the site, so it can’t appear in searches like “Diggin Connaught Place”.

    Fix: an outlet card or page for each location with address, hours, directions, call and “Reserve here”.

  7. Medium

    Wrong or missing dish photos

    Minestrone shows breadsticks with a dip. Smoked Chicken Soup shows a plated sliced-meat dish. Prawn Soup has no photo.

    Fix: match each photo to its dish, or use a clean text menu until real photos are ready.

  8. Medium

    Homepage opens behind “Swipe up to start the experience”

    The first screen is a full-bleed photo with a swipe prompt. The header has only the logo and Book Now, with no link to the menu or outlets. Guests have to work to find basic information.

    Fix: a first screen with the headline, rating, “Book a table” and “See menu”, plus clear navigation.

  9. Medium

    Different phone numbers on the website and on Google

    The website lists 90900 63232 (Diggin) and 96469 63696 (Gardin). The Google listing with 16,037 reviews shows 011 4080 0082. Mixed contact details confuse guests and weaken local search ranking.

    Fix: one consistent number per outlet on the website, Google and Instagram.

  10. Medium

    Search basics missing on the homepage

    No meta description, so Google writes its own snippet. No main heading (H1). The page title is just “Home | Diggin”.

    Fix: a title, description and heading that say what Diggin is and where, plus restaurant structured data for Google.

  11. Low

    No opening hours anywhere on the site

    “Are you open now?” is one of the most common questions guests ask before visiting.

    Fix: hours on each outlet card, kept in sync with Google.

  12. Low

    Small technical and accessibility errors

    The breadcrumb data sent to Google contains the untranslated label app.breadcrumbs.labels.home. Five homepage images have no description. Lighthouse also flags icon links with no name, low-contrast text and touch targets that are too small.

    Fix: handled as part of the rebuild. The demo scores 100 for accessibility.

Next steps

From demo to live site

  1. 20-minute walkthrough of the demo on your phone and laptop.
  2. Share the details: full menu and prices, outlet addresses and hours, your best photos, and the number that should receive bookings.
  3. Build and review: real photos and content go in, and you approve every page.
  4. Launch on diggin.co.in, with Google listings updated to match.
